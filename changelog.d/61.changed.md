DEFERRABLE` renders for any isolation level instead of only alongside `SERIALIZABLE`, `WAIT`/`NO WAIT` on `BEGIN`/`SET TRANSACTION` and `NO CACHE` on sequences and identity columns now raise `UnsupportedFeatureError` naming the option instead of being silently dropped or rendered, and a PostgreSQL 9 server asked for an identity column is refused instead of being handed `GENERATED ... AS IDENTITY` (the `SERIAL` type family remains the explicit pre-10 choice).
