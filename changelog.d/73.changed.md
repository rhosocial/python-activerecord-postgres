Join core's four-family introspection split and retire the backend-local probes it replaced: `IntrospectionSupport` is now `StructureIntrospectionSupport`, `OperationalStatsIntrospectionSupport`, `DataProfileIntrospectionSupport` and `DdlExtractionIntrospectionSupport`, each with an async twin; `supports_partition_info` and `supports_partition_introspection` are removed; and the `partitions` CLI family and `get_partition_state()` read core's `supports_partition_metadata_introspection` (PG 10+, the same predicate as `supports_table_partitioning`). `list_partitions()` is removed with no compatibility layer — `get_partition_state()` returns a `PartitionState` whose `PartitionBound` entries carry the structured RANGE (`lower`/`upper`), LIST (`values`) and HASH (`modulus`/`remainder`) facts plus `is_default`, while `raw["partitions"]` keeps the server's verbatim `{name, text}` spelling.
