START WITH`, the newly measured restrict and cache probes (`supports_schema_restrict`, `supports_restrict_view`, `supports_materialized_view_restrict`, `supports_truncate_restrict`, `supports_drop_function_restrict`, `supports_identity_cache`) and the routine probes are declared, and the master probes (`supports_materialized_cte`, `supports_truncate`, `supports_with_data_clause`, `supports_transaction_wait`) let core's formatters render `WITH [NO] DATA` on CTAS, `CREATE MATERIALIZED VIEW` and `REFRESH MATERIALIZED VIEW`; the naming side is stated once in the new `PostgresNamespaceMixin`/`PostgresNamespaceSupport`, which spells `[database.][schema.]name` level by level.
